PCI System Process Control Instructions
Chapter I. System Configuration
1.0 The process system composition:
The system according to the production process and function divided into 6 (Six) subsystems:
I. Raw Coal Storage and Transportation System
II. Hot Gas Generator (HGG) system
III. Coal Grinding & Pulverizing system
IV. Injection System
V. Utility System
VI.PC based Automation, Instrumentation, and Electrical System:
I. Raw Coal Storage and Transportation System
It includes Raw coal shed, Grab bucket crane, Raw coal hopper (1 no.), Vibro feeder, Belt feeder (A0102), charging conveyor (A0104), Iron remover (A0103), Metal Detector (OD-101), Chute Jam Switch, Steep angle conveyor (65 deg angle) along with belt safety devices, chute jam switch, Raw coal silo along with load cell (WT-301) and Radar Type level transmitter (LT-301), Air Gun etc.
A)Raw Coal Silo feeding system START & STOP Interlock:
RAW COAL SILO FEEDING SEQUENCE:
START Steep angle conveyor (SEQ-1)
START Charging Belt Conveyor with delay (SEQ-2)
START Belt feeder with delay (SEQ-3)
Rod gate of hopper (Manually Control) (SEQ-4)
Feed with grab Bucket (Manually Control) (SEQ-5)
Note: There will be two mode of operation AUTO (Remote) and MANUAL (Remote & Local)
AUTO Mode: SEQ-1to3 will be executed from HMI located at PCI Control room (Single Button) and SEQ-4&5 will be operated manually from field. STARTING shall be done by single button from HMI followed by control request from HMI
MANUAL Mode: There are two mode of operation i.e. Remote & Local
Remote Operation: will be operated from control room based on sequence and interlocks.
Local Operation: will be operated from field based on the permissive from HMI and individual equipment interlocks.
INTERLOCKS
STEEP ANGLE CONVEYOR:
1.0Raw coal silo level (LT_301) must be < 90 %.( Settable at higher level from HMI)
2.0Raw coal net weight (WT_301) must be < 100 Ton. (Settable at higher level from HMI.)
3.0There will be four mode of interlock.
i)Level Mode,
ii)Weight Mode,
iii)Level & Weight Mode,
iv) Level or Weight Mode.
In Level Mode only RCS level will be considered for interlock. In “Weight Mode” Mode only RCS weight will be considered for interlock. In Level & Weight Mode both RCS level & weight will be considered for interlock, in Level or Weight Mode either level or weight will be considered for interlock.

ii) Control on
iii) EPB Not Pressed
iv) L/R Selected from field (L-Local Mode, R-Remote Mode)
v) Run feed back
vi) Tripped

II. Hot Gas Generator (HGG) system
It includes Flame scanner (XT-203), Ignition system by using LPG, shut off valve (MSV-201), LPG manual isolation valve , NRV, Pressure switch (PS-201), Pressure gauge (PI-201),
BFG: Shut off valve (FSV-201), control valve (MCV-201), manual isolation valve, U-seal, moisture trapper, off-line moisture measurement system (MT-201), Pressure (PT-201), Flow (FT-201), temp. (TE-201) measurement.
Combustion air: Control valve (MCV-202), manual isolation valve, Pressure (PT-203), flow (FT-202), temp. (TE-203) measurement, mill inlet valve (MCV204).
Waste gas from bag filter outlet.
Bleed valve (MCV-203)
Emergency nitrogen valve (xxxx) (ANDE to provide Tag.No.) for HGG and mill inlet duct.
VRM inlet temp. Control loop and air fuel ratio control (FIC 201 & FIC 202)
HGG STARTING SEQUENCE
Before caring out any operation, Nitrogen Flushing (Nitrogen availability to be checked; pressure more than 6 Kg/Cm2, PT-401) from HGG inlet to VRM inlet to Bag filter to fine coal silo ensuring VRM inlet oxygen(AT-201) & CO (AT-202) bag filter inlet oxygen(AT-301) & CO (AT-302) Bag filter outlet oxygen(AT-305) & CO (AT-306) and fine coal silo oxygen(AT-307) & CO (AT-308) is less than 6%.
Nitrogen Flushing sequence
START Main ID Fan (C03021)
HGG inlet Valve Tag. No. (xxx) (ANDE to provide Tag.No.)
Close Valve bleed valve MCV-203, Mill inlet duct valve MCV-205 & stack outlet valve MCV-301 (Full close)
Open valve ID fan I/L Tag. No. MCV-302 (25% opening) & MCV-206 (Full Open)
HGG to VRM duct inlet nitrogen Valve Tag. No. (xxx)
Open mill inlet nitrogen valve (FSV-401)
Bag Filter inlet nitrogen valve (FSV-402)
Fine coal Silo inlet nitrogen valve (FSV-403)
Nitrogen Flushing will continue for 180 sec (Settable at higher level from HMI)
Ensure oxygen level at each point <6%.
Flushing Completed and closing the following valve FSV-401,402 & 403. Single button from HMI followed by control request from HMI.
HGG STARTING SEQUENCE
Before starting, break U-seal of HGG inlet of BFG line and monitor BFG pressure (PT-201)>700 mmWC and moisture trapper should be in line (Inlet, Outlet moisture Valve and inlet water valve shall be in open condition)
Nitrogen Flushing has been completed (Time delay-180sec AND Oxygen less than 6%)
Close mill inlet valve MCV-204 & 205
Open Bleed Valve MCV-203
START CA Fan
Monitor Combustion air Pressure (PT-203) >300 mmWC and BFG pressure (PT-201)>700 mmWC
START Ignition (single button) from HMI followed by control request from HMI.
Monitor LPG Pressure (PS-201) > 10000 mmWC , Open LPG Valve MSV-201
Monitor LPG Flame through flame scanner (XT-203)
(If LPG flame is not detected within 10 sec. ignition system to be STOP wait for 5 min restart the system)
Open BFG Shut off valve FSV-201
Switch ON CA & BFG flow control PID FIC201&202 with set point of CA 1500 Nm3/hr & ratio 70%
Monitor BFG Flame through flame scanner (XT-203) once flame has been detected STOP ignition system, wait for flame stability (Intensity > 80 %) through flame scanner (XT-203) once flame is stabilized wait for 10 sec and close LPG supply valve (MSV-201).
Increase set point of CA & BFG ratio as per process condition ensuring HGG inside temp (TE -203) < xxxx0C .
EMERGENCY ISOLATION CONDITION AND SEQUENCE OF ISOLATION OF HGG FOR HEATING CONDITION
Combustion air pressure (PT-203) <150 mmWC
BGF Pressure (PT-201) < 200 mmWC
CA Fan TRIP/ OFF
Flame off
HGG inside Temperature > 1000 deg. C
SEQUENCE
Close MSV-201 (LPG)
