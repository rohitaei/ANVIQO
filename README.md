# ANVIQO

## Industrial Plant Intelligence

ANVIQO is a read-only industrial intelligence platform designed to
help plant teams understand equipment condition, operational events,
maintenance priorities and management decisions.

### Product

ANVIQO Product V1.0

### Core capabilities

- Equipment intelligence
- Plant intelligence
- Event correlation
- Plant Brain
- Evidence and confidence
- Prediction verification
