# ANVIQO

## Industrial Plant Intelligence

ANVIQO is a read-only industrial intelligence platform designed to
help plant teams understand equipment condition, operational events,
maintenance priorities and management decisions.

### Product

ANVIQO Product V1.0

### Core capabilities

- Equipment intelligence
- Plant intelligence
- Event correlation
- Plant Brain
- Evidence and confidence
- Prediction verification
- Maintenance intelligence
- Shift intelligence
- Management intelligence
- Decision intelligence
- Executive / HOD intelligence

### Safety boundary

ANVIQO is recommendation-only.

READ-ONLY: TRUE
PLC WRITE: FALSE
SCADA CONTROL: FALSE
HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED: TRUE
AUTOMATIC AUTHORIZATION: FALSE
CAUSATION CLAIM: FALSE

ANVIQO does not automatically control plant equipment.

All operational decisions require qualified human verification
and appropriate plant safety procedures.
