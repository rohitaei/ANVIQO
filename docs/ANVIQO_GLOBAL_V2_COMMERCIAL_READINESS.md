# ANVIQO Global V2 — Commercial Readiness

## Product position
ANVIQO is positioned as the intelligence layer above existing industrial control and enterprise systems. It complements PLC/DCS/SCADA/historian/CMMS/ERP rather than replacing their control functions.

## Repeatable customer package
### PoV
- One plant
- Read-only telemetry
- Selected asset/area scope
- Evidence-backed ANVI
- What Changed
- alarms/events
- prediction/anomaly validation
- maintenance decision support
- measurable KPI baseline and outcome

### Subscription
- plant/site license
- telemetry/data volume tier
- intelligence capability tier
- user/role tier
- enterprise integration tier
- support/SLA tier

### Expansion
- additional plants
- enterprise command centre
- fleet benchmarking
- SAP/EAM/CMMS integrations
- advanced analytics and outcome learning

## Proof required before claiming commercial scale
- repeatable PoV results;
- reference customer;
- quantified operational outcome;
- deployment time measured;
- security review passed;
- reliability/SLA evidence;
- second-plant repeatability;
- documented onboarding process.

## Commercial principle
Do not promise a revenue number as a product capability. Revenue depends on customers, pricing, contracts, deployment capacity and retention.
